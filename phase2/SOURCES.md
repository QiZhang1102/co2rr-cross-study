# Data provenance

## Li et al. (2023) · acetate FE

- [Paper and data availability](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41929-023-01046-8) · [Public accepted manuscript](https://hub.hku.hk/bitstream/10722/340570/1/content.pdf)
- [Figure 2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41929-023-01046-8/figures/2) · [Figure 3](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41929-023-01046-8/figures/3) · [Supplementary Information](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41929-023-01046-8/MediaObjects/41929_2023_1046_MOESM1_ESM.pdf)
- The figures were downloaded from the publisher and the FE numbers were transcribed from the printed labels, without reading bar heights. Those labels are reported means from parallel measurements. The publisher's data availability statement points to an author request for the supporting data. Replicate-level measurements were not acquired. The file here contains only the numerical condition/FE facts used in this analysis.
- Figure 2c contributes four potential settings, Figure 3a five equilibration settings and Figure 3c four pressure settings. All use solution B (6 M KOH + 0.8 M H₃BO₃), the same reported catalyst preparation and a 1 h reaction. The shared 58 atm, 1 h pre-equilibration reference at −1.7 V vs Ag/AgCl (−0.91 V vs RHE at that reference) has 52.5% FE in all three panels and enters once. This yields 11 unique settings.
- Potential is represented as a scan offset from the reference in Figure 2c only. Figure 3a/c experiments use their stated RHE setting and receive zero scan offset. There is no cross-reference-scale conversion away from the anchor. Dissolved CO₂, pH after equilibration, current and product FE are not candidate inputs.

Supplementary PDF SHA-256 `3266ac09c4e49e8681ef40f63bc0fe2ce15760c64d4ede82352d76e9ae52f9b2`. Source images and PDF are not redistributed.

## Yang et al. (2022) · ethylene FE

- [Original study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-32740-z) · [Pinned workbook used in this analysis](https://github.com/Wang-Group/Cu-CO2RR_modifiers/blob/b9c7f420fed238cca9e1e3ac3e4010b09bd68ce9/CO2RRMolecularModification-HXR-20230323.xlsx)
- The numeric outcomes come from the Wang Group literature-curation workbook used in Phase 1. The original article identifies three bicentric copper complexes. For this secondary replay, the workbook's three exact modifier strings are treated as nominal categories and identified by worksheet order, without relying on a mapping between its strings and article compound names.
- Sheet1 rows 275–293, inclusive, contain 19 distinct modifier–potential combinations in the selected 0.1 M KHCO₃, neutral, H-cell block. Explicit numerical zero values are retained. The original FE values are taken as curated in the workbook and were not independently re-extracted from Yang et al. The raw workbook is downloaded and checksum-verified on reproduction.
