# Phase 2 protocol

This is a fixed-pool retrospective evaluation of sequential experiment selection for CO₂ electroreduction. Each candidate is an already measured condition. Its outcome is hidden until selected. The objective is the maximum reported Faradaic efficiency (FE, %) of one named product. Candidate generation and wet-lab experiments are outside this replay.

## Cohorts

- Primary cohort: Li et al., Nature Catalysis (2023), DOI 10.1038/s41929-023-01046-8. Transcribe every numeric acetate FE bar label in main Figures 2c, 3a and 3c, all within electrolyte B, the SW-Cu(OH)₂/Cu-derived cathode and a one-hour CO₂RR assay. Collapse the identical shared reference condition into one candidate. Use pressure, pre-equilibration duration and potential scan offset as candidate coordinates. In Fig. 2c the offset is from −1.7 V vs Ag/AgCl. The remaining sweeps fix the reported potential at −0.91 V vs RHE, the reference condition equivalent at 58 atm and 1 h. Their potential offset is zero by design and does not imply those settings equal −1.7 V vs Ag/AgCl under changed pH. See SOURCES.md.
- Public benchmark with numeric outcomes: Wang Group workbook, pinned commit b9c7f420fed238cca9e1e3ac3e4010b09bd68ce9, reporting data attributed to Yang et al., Nature Communications (2022), DOI 10.1038/s41467-022-32740-z. Target ethylene FE. The pool is the single 0.1 M KHCO₃, H-cell, neutral block with the largest number of distinct modifier–potential conditions with numeric target coverage in the workbook for ethylene. Hold paper, recorded cell type, electrolyte and pH category fixed. Search the workbook's explicitly measured modifier–potential pairs. Require a numeric RHE potential and FE within 0–100%, and a known modifier. Identical settings with repeated records count once; record all rows and average their FE. Never replace absent FE with zero. No outcome magnitudes enter cohort selection except range validation.

## Policies and evaluation

Use 500 seeded paired replays. Draw three initial candidates uniformly without replacement, with identical initial candidates for every policy in a replay. Compare uniform random sampling without replacement, GP posterior-mean greedy selection and GP expected improvement (EI) using the same Gaussian process. Use the same candidate ordering, initialization and query budget. Break acquisition ties using a uniformly permuted candidate order generated independently of outcomes. Record whether the best result is found after each budget. The fixed comparison budgets are six total conditions for the 11-point Li cohort and eight for the 19-point workbook cohort, including the initial three. Both are near half-pool budgets and are specified before running the replay. Report best-so-far FE, regret against the pool maximum, exact optimum hit rate, and paired differences. Report percentiles from a paired replay bootstrap as uncertainty over initializations and random ordering within this fixed pool, not over new chemistry.

Use a fixed Matérn 5/2 covariance on dimensionless continuous features scaled to the known candidate ranges and a separable categorical covariance where relevant. Use outcome-independent hyperparameters, fit only on observations revealed so far, and use positive fixed diagonal noise. Report length scale sensitivity at 0.20, 0.35 (primary), and 0.60. Inputs are controllable settings only. Report all policies and budgets even when improvements are absent.

Replay metrics are conditional on which experiments were published and entered the pool. A per-condition published FE summary can itself involve replicates, so a query counts one condition, not one physical electrolysis run. The evaluation cannot establish performance over unmeasured combinations or actual experimental savings.
